## What is changing
**Old requirement (the "NBR Rule," 89 FR 56028, July 8, 2024):**
- Covered nonbanks subject to covered public agency or court orders had to register with the Bureau. They had to submit the order and related information and keep it updated. Corrections were due within 30 days of learning of an inaccuracy.
- Bureau-supervised registered entities (with $5 million or more in annual receipts, among other criteria) also had to designate an "attesting executive." That executive signed an annual written statement on order compliance. The document says the rule required this for at least 10 years and that supporting records had to be kept for five years.
- The Bureau could publish the registration information.

**New requirement:** All of this is rescinded. The rule removes the registration requirements (§§ 1092.202-.203), the written-statement requirements (§ 1092.204), the publication provisions (§ 1092.205), subpart A, §§ 1092.200-.201 and .206, and appendix A. The Bureau cited cost-benefit concerns, duplication of NMLS and other public sources, and the cost of maintaining the system.

**Interim background:** The document notes a prior April 11, 2025 Bureau announcement that it would not prioritize enforcement or supervision for missed future deadlines under the NBR Rule.
