## What is changing
The IFR rescinds the 2021 COVID RESPA Rule (86 FR 34848, June 30, 2021). The text does not reproduce the prior wording of the amended provisions, so the "before" below comes from the preamble and the removal instructions.

- **§ 1024.31:** removes the definition of "COVID-19-related hardship."
- **§ 1024.39 (early intervention):**
  - Paragraph (a), live contact, is revised. The text shown requires live contact or good-faith efforts by the 36th day of delinquency, and again within 36 days after each later payment due date while the borrower remains delinquent. The servicer must also promptly inform the borrower about loss mitigation options, if appropriate.
  - Paragraph (e) is removed. The preamble says the temporary additional live-contact requirements ran only until October 1, 2022.
- **§ 1024.41 (loss mitigation):**
  - Paragraph (c)(2)(i) is revised. The exceptions it references are now (c)(2)(ii), (iii), and (v).
  - Paragraph (c)(2)(vi) is removed.
  - Paragraph (c)(2)(v)(A)(1) is revised.
  - Paragraph (f)(3), the temporary COVID-19 loss mitigation procedural safeguards, is removed. The preamble says these did not apply if the first foreclosure notice or filing was made on or after January 1, 2022.
- **Supplement I (Official Interpretations):** comment 39(a) is revised, and comment 41(b)(1) is revised. The comments on 41(f)(3) and 41(f)(3)(ii)(C), "Unresponsive Borrower," are removed.
- **Stated rationale:** the safeguards sunset by their own terms, and the Bureau also says the provisions "needlessly complicate Regulation X without commensurate benefits." The Bureau states the rule imposes no costs on consumers or covered persons.

**Ambiguity to flag:** the revised § 1024.41(c)(2)(v)(A)(1) still refers to "COVID-19-related hardship" and to a forbearance program under the Coronavirus Economic Stabilization Act, section 4022. The definition of that term in § 1024.31 is removed. The preamble describes the rule as removing COVID-19 language, but the text does not explain how these remaining references operate without the definition. I have not resolved this.

## Does it apply to this servicer?
**In scope, with limited operational change expected.**
- The amendments are to Regulation X, Subpart C, mortgage servicing. The document draws no distinction by institution type or portfolio size.
- As a bank with more than 5,000 loans, the servicer has no size- or type-based carve-out in this text.
- The document does not mention any small servicer exemption. That is not stated in the document, and the servicer's more-than-5,000-loan profile should be confirmed against the rule's coverage.
- The Bureau's § 1022 analysis says the rule has no unique impact on insured depository institutions with less than $10 billion in assets. This does not bear on this servicer's scope, and the servicer's asset size is not given.

## What is not changing
- The core live-contact obligation in § 1024.39(a) remains: 36 days, repeated after each payment due date while delinquent, with prompt notice of loss mitigation availability if appropriate. The revised comments continue to say these items are unchanged in substance: good-faith efforts, leaving a recorded message does not count as live contact, and the servicer's discretion on "if appropriate."
- The comments still reference the written early intervention notice under § 1024.39(b)(1), which is not shown as changed.
- The § 1024.41 requirement not to evade evaluation of a complete application remains. The exceptions in (c)(2)(ii), (iii), and (v) remain.
- The comment 41(b)(1) guidance on reasonable diligence, when an inquiry becomes an application, and information outside the borrower's control remains. This includes the short-term forbearance and repayment-plan diligence guidance tied to § 1024.41(c)(2)(iii).
- The 2024 proposed rule (89 FR 60204) on streamlined loss mitigation is not finalized by this document. It is described only as a proposal, and the Bureau says it would consider the comments received in a future rulemaking.
- Other Regulation X provisions not mentioned are untouched.
