## What is changing
**Old:** The 2021 COVID RESPA Rule (86 FR 34848, June 30, 2021) added:
- Temporary procedural safeguards tied to foreclosure. They did not apply if the first foreclosure notice or filing came on or after January 1, 2022.
- Temporary additional early-intervention live-contact requirements, required only until October 1, 2022.
- A temporary allowance for servicers to offer certain loan modifications to borrowers with a "COVID-19-related hardship."
- Related definitions and commentary.

The document says these provisions had already sunset by their own terms.

**New:** The IFR removes that COVID-19 language from Regulation X:
- **§ 1024.31:** removes the definition of "COVID-19-related hardship."

- **§ 1024.41:**
