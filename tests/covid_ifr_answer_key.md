# Answer key: COVID-19 Reg X rescission

## Facts the tool must get right
- Document type and status: Interim final rule - Protections for Borrowers Affected by the COVID-19 Emergency Under the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA), Regulation X (critical)
- Publication date: May 16, 2025
- Effective date: July 15, 2025 (critical)
- Compliance date: Not Stated
- Comment deadline: June 16, 2025

## Applicability
- Bank, more than 5,000 loans: In scope. Reg X servicing rules apply regardless of institution type. Relief, not a new obligation: removes temporary COVID provisions
- Nonbank, more than 5,000 loans: In scope. Same as bank; the document draws no distinction by institution type.
- Small servicer (5,000 or fewer): Little or no practical impact. Small servicers are already exempt from most Reg X loss mitigation and early intervention procedures, so most of the rescinded provisions likely did not apply to them. Tool should flag this as something to verify rather than state it with certainty

## What is not changing
- The anti-evasion exception for COVID-19 deferral and partial claim options in 1024.41(c)(2)(v) stays in force. It came from a separate 2020 interim final rule and is not rescinded by this document. (critical)
- The permanent Reg X servicing rules (general early intervention and loss mitigation requirements) remain. Only the temporary 2021 COVID amendments are removed.
- Record retention obligations are unchanged. Servicers should keep records showing compliance while the 2021 rule was in effect.
- The small servicer exemption is unchanged.

## Expected priority
- High, per the current prompt rule (already in effect).
- My view: Medium, since this is cleanup of expired provisions


## Things the tool must not do
- Invent a compliance date, or present the comment deadline as a compliance date (critical)
- Describe it as a proposed rule or a regular final rule instead of an interim final rule
- Treat the rescission as a new obligation rather than relief (critical)
- Say the 1024.41(c)(2)(v) COVID deferral and partial claim exception was rescinded (critical)
- Suggest the comment period is still open (it closed June 16, 2025)
- Recommend deleting records of compliance with the 2021 rule
- Cite section numbers, law firm commentary, or other facts not in the document text
- State the small servicer impact as certain without flagging it to verify
